About NeuronCodec

NeuronCodec is an open-source foundation team focusing on neuroscience, biotech, and brain-computer interfaces. NeuronCodec is researching the integration of technology and biology, with a focus on neuroscience.

Mission

Mission

NeuronCodec's mission is to achieve not only reading the communication data within synapses and a neuron network, but also to apply write actions into memory. The NeuronCodec foundation is also passionate about any integration that a tech stack can bring into a biology stack. Beyond that, NeuronCodec is passionate about automating laboratories with AI agents and boosting research efficiency. The foundation believes the combination of AI, technology, and biology will shape a very promising future for the human race.
